## What are Sitelinks?
Sitelinks are additional links that appear below the main search result in Google, helping users navigate directly to specific pages or sections of your website. They appear automatically when Google determines your site is authoritative and well-structured.

## How to Optimize for Sitelinks

### 1. Internal Linking Structure
- Ensure all important pages are linked from the homepage
- Use descriptive anchor text for internal links
- Create a clear site hierarchy

### 2. Page Titles and Meta Descriptions
- Use descriptive, keyword-rich page titles
- Write compelling meta descriptions
- Include location keywords (Dabgram, Siliguri)

### 3. Content Quality
- Provide unique, valuable content on each page
- Use proper heading structure (H1, H2, H3)
- Include relevant keywords naturally

### 4. Technical SEO
- Ensure fast loading times
- Make site mobile-friendly
- Use proper URL structure
