## Setup in Google Ads

1. Go to **Ads & assets** → **Extensions** → **Sitelinks**.
2. Add each sitelink with the **Link text** and **Final URL** above.
3. Add both description lines in the sitelink description fields (helps relevance and can improve ad strength).
4. You can use up to 4 sitelinks per ad (up to 6 in some cases). Prioritise: **Book Appointment**, **Services & Pricing**, **View Prices**, **Contact Us**.

Your site is a single-page app with hash sections (`#booking`, `#services`, etc.), so these URLs will open the right section when users click from search ads.
