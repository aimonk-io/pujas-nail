<p>Beautiful, strong acrylic nails — done at your home, at your convenience. Puja's Nail Studio brings professional-grade acrylic nail extension service to your doorstep anywhere in Siliguri, West Bengal.</p>

<h2>What Are Acrylic Nail Extensions?</h2>
<p>Acrylic nails are created by combining a liquid monomer and powder polymer to form a hard, durable coating over your natural nails or nail tips. They are the most popular nail extension type in India because of their strength, versatility, and affordability. Acrylic nails can be shaped into any length — square, almond, stiletto, coffin — and decorated with any nail art design.</p>

<h2>Our Acrylic Nail Services in Siliguri</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Full Set</strong> – Brand new acrylic nail extensions on all 10 fingers</li>
  <li><strong>Infill / Refill</strong> – Maintenance fill for grown-out acrylics (every 2–3 weeks)</li>
  <li><strong>Nail Art on Acrylics</strong> – Custom designs, glitter, ombré, French tip, 3D art</li>
  <li><strong>Acrylic Removal</strong> – Safe, professional removal without damaging natural nails</li>
  <li><strong>Repair</strong> – Fixing a broken or lifted acrylic nail</li>
</ul>

<h2>How Long Do Acrylic Nails Last?</h2>
<p>Acrylic nails last 2–3 weeks before needing an infill. With good care — avoiding harsh chemicals, wearing gloves for cleaning, and moisturising cuticles — they can last up to 4 weeks. We recommend booking an infill appointment every 2–3 weeks to keep your nails looking fresh and to prevent lifting.</p>

<h1>Gel Nail Extensions at Home in Siliguri</h1>

<p>Gel nails offer the most natural-looking nail extension available. Lightweight, flexible, and chip-resistant — Puja's Nail Studio brings professional gel nail extension service to your home in Siliguri.</p>

<h2>What Are Gel Nails?</h2>
<p>Gel nails use a gel-based product cured under a UV or LED lamp to create a strong, glossy finish on your nails. Unlike acrylics, gel nails are odourless, feel lighter and more natural, and cause less damage to the natural nail. They are the preferred choice for people who want a subtle, elegant look for everyday wear or special occasions.</p>

<h2>Our Gel Nail Services in Siliguri</h2>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Gel Extension Full Set</strong> – New gel nail extensions with your chosen shape and length</li>
  <li><strong>Gel Overlay</strong> – Gel coating over your natural nails for strength and shine</li>
  <li><strong>Gel Polish Manicure</strong> – Long-lasting gel polish on natural nails, lasts 2–3 weeks</li>
  <li><strong>Gel Nail Art</strong> – Designs, gradients, glitter, chrome powder on gel nails</li>
  <li><strong>Gel Removal</strong> – Safe soak-off removal with no damage</li>
  <li><strong>Gel Infill</strong> – Maintenance fill for grown-out gel extensions</li>
</ul>

<h2>Gel vs Acrylic — Which Is Right for You?</h2>
<p>Gel nails are ideal if you want a natural, lightweight feel and are okay with slightly shorter wear time. Acrylic nails are better if you want extra strength, longer lengths, or more dramatic nail art. Not sure? We can recommend the best option based on your lifestyle and nail condition during your appointment.</p>

<h2>How Long Do Gel Nails Last?</h2>
<p>Gel extensions last 3–4 weeks. Gel polish manicures on natural nails last 2–3 weeks without chipping. Gel nails tend to grow out more gracefully than acrylics, making them low maintenance between appointments.</p>
